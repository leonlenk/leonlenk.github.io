Hi I'm Leon Lenk, I'm currently a Research Fellow at the [Supervised Program for Alignment Research](https://sparai.org/) investigating whether reinforcement learning elicits or generates abilities. I often think of my motivations in terms of [shard theory](https://turntrout.com/shard-theory), thus my website is organized such that each piece is a "shard" of my motivations. Currently I'm interested in x-risk from AI and encompassing theories of how LLMs work (though that's somewhat fallen out of favor).Outside of AIS research I like to draw, write, and play minecraft.
